# Lazurite is a blue rock like material, it was a precious material.

So as the founder likes the color blue, thus Lazursoft was made. 

It took a while to realize, but I do in fact enjoy solving problems in hardware and software. And instead of chasing an imaginary role in an office, I enjoyed being out on call and troubleshooting all the ways data flows.

So after freelancing for a while, I decided to start this small enterprise, where anyone from a frustrated worker dealing with a busted printer, a startup looking for assisstance, or someone needing help with software can seek assisstance.

Most people don't need full time support staff for most of the time, but sometimes their ways aren't working.

Lazursoft will create software with first hand knowlege of what users want and need, by working with users and helping them firsthand (Not by spyware).

Automation and technology don't need to be the enemies of humanity, if we choose they can help us discover a lot.

-Ryan Joseph McCurdy, Lazursoft founder.
