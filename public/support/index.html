Convientent assisstance

Lazursoft isn’t above fixing hardware or software problems.

We have solved issues in everywhere from data centers and offices to active retail spaces.

Support services provided by Lazursoft include:

  1. General assistance: When everything that usually works by itself stops working, we thrive in making it work again.
    Example cases:

    Guest devices can’t connect to the customer’s wireless network.
    Fixing a used printer that doesn’t work.

    • Remote: 15,000 won; in-person 30,000 won for 1 hour of work

  2. Industry assistance: For the larger more time consuming cases.
    Example case:

    Installing 1 ordering kiosk in person in Seoul, north of the Han river.

    • In person 60,000 won initial fee + 10,000 won for additional hour.

  3. South Korean tech buyers market assistance: help seek out and purchase items - in Seoul, in person only. Example case:

    Help buying a used 300,000 won Macbook on Dang-gun market(당근마겟) delivered 3 km away from home office:

    350,000 won total: 30,000 won finders fee + 15,000 won transport fee (5,000 won per kilometer) + 2020 MacBook Air price (300,000)

*prices and services are negoitable and not indicative of the current market. Contact Lazursoft for more information.
