Operational assisstance

Services are continuously in movement, they don’t always move by themselves.

Anything from a mission critical cloud service disconnecting to a printer malfunction can be a costly problem.

Available in in person(Norther Seoul area), or remotely. All prices displayed are estimates and subject to change.

Support services provided by Lazursoft include:

Maintaining physical devices (northern Seoul/Uijeongbu only): Promise to answer on call and troubleshoot devices

Monthly remote check in: a monthly check using remote desktop control. to see if your projects are running and safe. (20,00 won a month)

Cloud assistance: Helping your business provide an accessible web service and data on major cloud providers. (20,000 an hour for 1 hour consultation)

Cloud maintenance: Run a maintained service on a local server in korea, monthly update report sent. (30,000 won a month for a 100m/s service focused on 1 nation) Global server with monthly update. (50,000 won a month for a highly available service) a report is provided along with a ledger.

Additional maintenance and emergency services are subject to fees.
